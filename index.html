Governance Framework for AI Deployment

Make governance verifiable, not asserted.

Current AI ethics frameworks produce documents. These documents are asserted at deployment, interpreted elastically in operation, and quietly revised after failure. This framework changes that — using cryptographic hashing and blockchain publication to make the standards an AI system is governed by a matter of public, tamper-evident record.
